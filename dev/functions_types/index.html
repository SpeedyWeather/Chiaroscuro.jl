Chiaroscuro.evaluate — Method
evaluate(
    albedo::ChiaroscuroAlbedo,
    simulation::SpeedyWeather.AbstractSimulation;
    reference
) -> NamedTuple{(:score, :land_score, :ocean_score, :tropical_score, :polar_score), <:NTuple{5, Any}}

Evaluate the surface air temperature on the last time step of simulation that used the albedo against a reference surface temperature. Returns a NamedTuple of area-weighted root mean square errors [K] globally (score) and over land, ocean, the tropics (30˚S-30˚N) and polar regions (poleward of 60˚N/S). Land and ocean are weighted by the land fraction of each grid cell.

source
Chiaroscuro.reference_surface_temperature — Method
reference_surface_temperature(grid::AbstractGrid) -> Any

Made-up reference surface temperature [K] on grid until the real reference is provided: 300K at the equator decreasing to 250K at the poles.

Chiaroscuro.rmse — Method
rmse(field, reference, weights, mask) -> Any

Area-weighted root mean square error between field and reference, with an additional weight mask (e.g. 1 inside and 0 outside a region, or the land fraction) per grid point.
