Chiaroscuro leaderboard

The Chiaroscuro leaderboard ranks submissions in /submissions to the Chiaroscuro albedo challenge by their score, the root mean square error (RMSE) of the simulated surface temperature against a reference, see Evaluation. Lower is better! The land, ocean, tropical and polar scores are the same RMSE but only evaluated over the respective region, see Regional scores. The generalisation score is the RMSE over the western hemisphere (180˚-360˚E) only, the half of the globe for which no reference data is given to participants, see Generalisation score. It shows how well an albedo generalises to unseen regions. To Submit to Chiaroscuro see that section of the docs and particular the rules and formalities explained within. Also see List of submissions for a list of all submissions, their respective code and albedo maps.
